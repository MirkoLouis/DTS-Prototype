 **Chapter 1**  
**The Problem and Its Background**

**1\. Introduction**

* A general overview of the research topic.  
* Context and background of the problem (what’s happening in the real world).  
* Why the study is important in your field (IT/IS, education, etc.).

**1.2. Background of the Study**

* More detailed discussion of the issue.  
* Related situations/problems faced by the institution or users.  
* Sets the stage for why your solution is needed.

**1.3. Statement of the Problem**

* The main research problem in clear terms.  
* Often divided into general problem and specific problems (which become your research objectives).

**1.4. Objectives of the Study**  
**General Objective:** 

* The overall purpose of the study.

**Specific Objectives**

*  These goals are specific and measurable, often aligned with the features of the system or its evaluation criteria.

**1.5. Significance of the Study**

* Who will benefit from the study and how (students, faculty, offices, stakeholders, researchers, community).  
* Practical and academic contributions.

**1.6. Scope and Delimitation**  
**Scope**

* what the system/study *covers* (features, functions, target users).

  **Delimitation**

  *  what the study *does not* cover (limitations, exclusions, constraints).

**1.7. Definition of Terms**  
Define key technical or study-specific terms for clarity
